Keras MSE averages over the outputs, so its gradient is 2/nout times ours: 2× larger for XOR (1 output), 2/7 for the 7-segment net. With our loss ½·Σdiff², TensorFlow follows our curve to about 1e-5. That shows both implementations compute the same backpropagation.
